ABOUT

ReSTEMblance, LLC is a DevHouse (developer house) that focuses on Computer Science Education = the why and how of computing and Web Development = one of the what you can do with it.

Our mission: Real STEM is Hands-On™

Our vision: Using STEM related content to influence and enhance STEM careers.

Who We Are

ReSTEMblance is a full-service DevHouse dedicated to building and maintaining powerful, reliable, and scalable technology solutions. Our team blends strategy, design, and evaluation to help organizations strengthen their digital presence.

Dr. Cristal Harris, Ed.D, is the owner and developer of ReSTEMblance, LLC. She is an educator and STEM innovator specializing in computational thinking and computer science education, with a focus on equity and culturally responsive teaching.

We establish a digital handprint for organizations through technology solutions that support sustainable STEM learning and empower the next generation of digital leaders.
