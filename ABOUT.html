ABOUT

ReSTEMblance, LLC is a DevHouse (developer house) that focuses on Computer Science Education = the why and how of computing and Web Development = one of the what you can do with it.

Our mission: Real STEM is Hands-On™

Our vision: Using STEM related content to influence and enhance STEM careers.

Who We Are

ReSTEMblance is a full-service DevHouse dedicated to building and maintaining powerful, reliable, and scalable technology solutions. Our team blends strategy, design, and evaluation to ensure that your solution is secure, maintainable and scalable for your business. We create unique and innovative pathways for individuals to explore computer science education, making complex technology accessible and engaging for all.

Dr. Cristal Harris, Ed.D, is the owner and developer of ReSTEMblance, LLC. Dr. Harris is an educator and STEM innovator specializing in computational thinking and computer science education. Her expertise spans K-12 education, higher education, corporate training, and professional development.

What we do as a DevHouse is establish a digital handprint for businesses and organizations through technology solutions and web development, that support our cause for sustainable, hands-on STEM education.

How we help

We do the work for you to design your website. We cater to your organization's needs by building a website using leading technology that will expand your organization's vision. We offer web design and development solutions that create lasting impressions and improve business growth. Our team will design your website with a user-friendly interface, visually appealing layouts, and strong functionality.

How we expand

Real STEM is hands-on. ReSTEMblance's research and development of STEM learning activities using a computing education curriculum aspires learners to build their interest in STEM fields. We facilitate learning through hands-on activities that help educators teach and students learn computational thinking and computer science concepts in the classroom, afterschool, or homeschool settings. We empower educators and expand students' connection to computer science, coding, and digital citizenship.
